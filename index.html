Produce delivery scheduling

Delivery requests are not available here yet

NASMS exposes a produce-order request with a response status and scheduled date, but its service does not verify that the supplied National ID belongs to the authenticated farmer. The order-history route also declares a path variable that is missing from its URL. These delivery actions are withheld until the backend can safely scope them to the current farmer.

Loan balances and payment records

Remaining balances and any payment records included in your loan response appear here. NASMS exposes M-Pesa STK Push as a loan-linked action.
